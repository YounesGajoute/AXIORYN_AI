# AXIORYN AI — Project Charter
**Baseline:** GOV-1.0 | **Date:** 2026-10-19 | **Status:** Governance initialization

## Mission
Build an extensible enterprise multi-agent AI platform. The first production module is **AXIORYN AI Sales Team** for industrial B2B sales workflows.

## Confirmed scope
- AXIORYN AI Core: reusable orchestration, integration, governance and observability foundations.
- Sales Team: LinkedIn marketing, competitor intelligence, lead discovery, buying committee mapping, CRM, personalized outreach and intelligent follow-up.
- Engineering supervision: requirements traceability, agent reports, technical reviews, acceptance gates and production readiness.

## Exclusions and constraints
- **No software implementation during project initialization.**
- No claim that architecture, provider choices, deployment infrastructure or security controls are approved without owner evidence.
- No automated external outreach or access to private data without authorization and compliance review.

## Success criteria
1. Owner-approved scope and acceptance criteria.
2. Auditable mapping from requirements to implementation and tests.
3. Modular interfaces between Core and Sales Team.
4. Security, privacy and operational gates passed with evidence.
5. Release approved by the owner.

## Roles
- Owner: product priorities, access, decisions and final approvals.
- Governance assistant: architecture oversight, requirement control, risk and acceptance review.
- Coding agent: changes only after explicit authorization, evidence-backed reports.
