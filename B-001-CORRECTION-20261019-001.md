## PROPOSED

1. Reconcile the architecture as four distinct logical layers: Experience and
   Control Center; AXIORYN AI Core runtime; Sales domain and CAI Sales Manager
   commercial orchestration; and policy-enforced connectors/data boundaries.
2. Keep Core responsible for generic execution, security, policy enforcement,
   task lifecycle, and observability. Keep CAI Sales Manager responsible for
   commercial strategy and domain decisions. Neither component substitutes for
   the other.
3. Treat Agent 01 as the bounded sales-domain coordination role within CAI Sales
   Manager, recommended to be renamed **CAI Sales Manager / Commercial
   Orchestrator** to avoid a duplicate "Executive Director." Treat Agent 09 as
   the follow-up and account-strategy specialist that supplies recommendations
   to CAI Sales Manager; it is not the parent orchestrator.
4. Do not create an additional Executive Director component by implication. If
   the owner wants a separate executive role, its scope, authority, relation to
   Sales Manager, and boundary from Core must be decided in an ADR first.
5. Add requirement proposals `SM-REQ-001`–`SM-REQ-010` and backlog proposals
   `B-023`–`B-032` as separate, versioned additions. Retain `R-001`–`R-026` and
   `B-001`–`B-022` unchanged. Detailed proposals are in
   [Master Requirements](./MASTER_REQUIREMENTS.md),
   [Development Backlog](./DEVELOPMENT_BACKLOG.md), and
   [System Architecture](./SYSTEM_ARCHITECTURE.md).
6. Record the proposed reconciliation as `ADR-SM-001`, status
   `PROPOSED_PENDING_OWNER_APPROVAL`; no decision is effective until the owner
   approves it.
