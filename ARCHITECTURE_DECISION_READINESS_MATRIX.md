## Purpose
This matrix records the open architecture decisions that remain pending owner approval. It is a governance artifact only and does not authorize implementation, external connectivity, or production use. Every open decision includes an accountability and dependency record so that the package can be reviewed, prioritized, and resolved without implying acceptance.

## Decision registry dependencies and relationships
- D-001 (identity source) and D-002 (tenant model) are prerequisites to D-003 (RBAC), D-005 (audit retention), and D-009 (data governance).
- D-006 (integration allowlist) is prerequisite to D-007 (LinkedIn), D-008 (CRM), and D-010 (secrets).
- D-004 (approval delegation) must be resolved before any external outbound or side-effecting action is approved.
- D-011 (formal contracts) and D-012 (routing/budget) are follow-on decisions that depend on the governance boundary and audit structures being resolved.
